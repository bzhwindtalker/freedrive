# Freedrive News Log

A lightweight changelog-style digest from the main forum development thread:
https://foil.zone/t/light-assist-season-3

## 2026-10-27
- **#377** Season board proven: a "sinker" board under 50 L rides fine. Upwind angles are hard. **Turbine hardware ordered: 5065 320 KV (rated 1800 W) + 8 mm stainless shaft.** Turbine CAD paused meanwhile.

## 2026-10-18
- **#373** Turbine CAD progress posted: two-stage nacelle section with centre cone and converging exit.
- **#374 / #375 / #376** Drag and pop-up debate with @Jan12. Two-stage impeller logic recorded: stage one moves water, stage two moves the air-water mix.

## 2026-10-17
- **#372** High aspect 980 cm² foil will not start the sinker board, even at 60 A. Board sinks, so it needs volume. Conclusion: big low-aspect wings for learning starts.

## 2026-10-13
- **#369** Files bought for an **RC boat 40 mm turbine**. Builder report: **13.3 kg thrust, 8 s, 8S / 2.2 kW**. Plan: scale to **50 mm** for a **55 mm** package. Layout: motor under the mast, one water inlet on each side, battery in front. 5055 motor considered for a small unit; a bigger one if a 55 mm package is acceptable.

## 2026-09-28
- **#361 / #363 / #366** Controller teardown after one season: **Magic Gel potting dissolved the silicone thermal pad** down to 2 mm. Chemical reaction, not heat. Rule: keep potting off thermal pads.

## 2026-08-17
- **#342** Machining cost data point: aluminium printed/machined in China about **500 €** versus **1100 USD** for the same part in steel in the USA.

## 2026-08-10 to 2026-08-18
- **#323 / #330 / #336 / #338 / #339 / #340** Folding hub cracking becomes the main reliability topic. Two 5085 hubs broken (PA12, then PLA+ remix), Ti blades lost in the water. Materials data: PBT-GF cracked, PC-ABS trialled, SUNLU Easy PA with three bolts through the base holding, SiraTech PPA-CF holding. Hammer test at 90 °C: PETG better than PETG-CF, Easy PA only deforms.
- **#327** Power raised on the 5085 setup: phase limit 130 → **150 A**, battery limit 50 → **55 A**, LVC **2.5 V per cell** under load, erpm **+10 %**. 1050 cm² foil then underpowered on sag.
- **#322** Session data: 9.69 km in 2 h 11 min, about 30 waves, on 12S1P P42 + 1300 cm² foil.
- **#332 / #334 / #335 / #338** RF: **868 MHz (BRemote)** keeps range through water. A smaller 868 MHz RX announced. Working fix: longer coax, sealed antenna ends, antennas lined up. Better idea: antenna inside the TPU pad.
- **#344** Remote mod for pop-up: low trigger plus a finger guard with a flat press pad. No disassembly needed.

## 2026-10-05
- **#368** S4 board bottom glassed (wet lay, 2 × 125 gr glass + 200 gr carbon patch over dual stringer). Final board target ~4 kg.
- **#367** S4 board shaping progress photos.

## 2026-10-01
- **#364** S4 dedicated board build started: 163×52×80 mm EPS blank, no trench (parawing capability kept), dual stringer layout.

## 2026-09-17
- **#358** Dedicated S4 system concept v1: 95 mm 5080 jet, 12S2P removable 21700 battery (sealed, travel-fillable with 4S1P + wires), in-board cartridge intake in front of the mast. Board: dual plywood stringers or carbon tube, electronics on top.

## 2026-09-13
- **#355** **First water test of the S4 jet drive (turbine): success.** Small board starts (full battery only), big board flies with pumping. Max draw 41V/65A ≈ 2.66 kW. Motor-off touchdown = high drag, motor-on = clean. Air intake very sensitive.
- **#356** Test videos posted: [13 septembre 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBwcKk_be1A), [End of battery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXS7Xq_WdP4).

## 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-12
- **#354** Early jet feedback: very sensitive to air intake, balance OK, no scraping/vibration.
- **#353** @V_S: 5080 prop is low pitch (~95 mm) — good thrust, low speed.
- **#351** Jet rationale confirmed: safety (prop injury risk), DW + surf pop-up + displacement motoring.
- **#348** S4 jet concept detail: angled intake to save space and reduce cavitation. 5080 motor with supplied 4-blade prop.
- **#345** S4 jet concept first posted ("trying something new for season 4").
