# Freedrive News Log

A lightweight changelog-style digest from the main forum development thread:
https://foil.zone/t/light-assist-season-3

## 2026-10-05
- **#368** S4 board bottom glassed (wet lay, 2 × 125 gr glass + 200 gr carbon patch over dual stringer). Final board target ~4 kg.
- **#367** S4 board shaping progress photos.

## 2026-10-01
- **#364** S4 dedicated board build started: 163×52×80 mm EPS blank, no trench (parawing capability kept), dual stringer layout.

## 2026-09-17
- **#358** Dedicated S4 system concept v1: 95 mm 5080 jet, 12S2P removable 21700 battery (sealed, travel-fillable with 4S1P + wires), in-board cartridge intake in front of the mast. Board: dual plywood stringers or carbon tube, electronics on top.

## 2026-09-13
- **#355** **First water test of the S4 jet drive (turbine): success.** Small board starts (full battery only), big board flies with pumping. Max draw 41V/65A ≈ 2.66 kW. Motor-off touchdown = high drag, motor-on = clean. Air intake very sensitive.
- **#356** Test videos posted: [13 septembre 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aBwcKk_be1A), [End of battery](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OXS7Xq_WdP4).

## 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-12
- **#354** Early jet feedback: very sensitive to air intake, balance OK, no scraping/vibration.
- **#353** @V_S: 5080 prop is low pitch (~95 mm) — good thrust, low speed.
- **#351** Jet rationale confirmed: safety (prop injury risk), DW + surf pop-up + displacement motoring.
- **#348** S4 jet concept detail: angled intake to save space and reduce cavitation. 5080 motor with supplied 4-blade prop.
- **#345** S4 jet concept first posted ("trying something new for season 4").

## 2026-08-17
- **#342** V4 prop pod lost in the water. Reliability rework started. 5085 motor re-ordered.

## 2026-06-15
- **#261** Ti prop test on 5085: perfect out of the box, very smooth when pumping. Previous DW score passed on 15 kt tailwind + slow Saône current.

## 2026-06-06
- **#256** Titanium blade set received (90 USD, alu + ti). Zero post-processing, edges sharper than alu. Hubs in PPA-GF 100% infill.

## 2026-05-16
- **#220** 5085 first water test (unbalanced carbon prop, cotter pins): felt stronger than maytech 6374, lower drag on pod touchdown, easy starts in small chop.
- **#216** Printed carbon prop failed (zero impregnation). Ordered two printed prop sets + one titanium set.

## 2026-05-12 to 2026-05-13
- **#211** 5085 pod hack in progress. SUP flat-start training log: 80% success on Condor M and Barracuda XL, goal 1000 cm² foil by end of year.
- **#208** 5085 motor ordered (same as @foilstate's design), ~400 g savings.

## 2026-02-27
- **#153** Community feedback: strong positive response to the project direction.
- **#152** Clarified build path: no CNC required; major parts can be printed at home or ordered from print services.

## 2026-02-26
- **#150** Community build assembled in Corsica with RS50 tabless cells (5.15Ah) and custom blade testing.
- **#149** Field-proven sealing + reliability updates: EVA foam gasket success and switch toward titanium screws after stainless M8 issues.

## 2026-02-23 to 2026-02-25
- **#142–#145** Active community experimentation on sealed battery compartment concepts and foaming TPU gasket materials.

## 2026-02-18
- **#140** Ride feedback on high motor position (blades ~20mm from board): better for pumping/weight centralization, harder upwind in chop.
